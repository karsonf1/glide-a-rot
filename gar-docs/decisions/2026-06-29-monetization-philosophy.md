# Monetization Philosophy

**Decided:** 2026-06-29 (logged retroactively)

## Question

Should paid features in [Glide-A-Rot](../_index.md) gatekeep progression, or only accelerate progress free players can already make?

## Decision

**Acceleration, not gatekeeping.** Free players can fully progress through every system given enough time. Paid options only speed things up.

## Why

Long-term player trust and retention matter more for a solo-dev game relying on organic growth and word-of-mouth than short-term revenue maximization does.

There's also a structural argument specific to this game: [GAR Social Mechanic](../systems/social-mechanic.md) is designed so that server population *improves everyone's rarity rolls*. A gatekeeping model suppresses population in exchange for extracting more from fewer players — which, in a game whose signature feature rewards having people around, is self-defeating on its own terms.

## Concrete levers that fit

Crate cooldown reduction · crate timers · fuel and timer skips · cosmetic skins · glider unlocks that are also earnable.

Cosmetics are the cleanest fit: they monetize expression rather than power.

## The line

Some pay-to-win is intentional and acceptable. What's not permitted is a purchase being the **only** path to any piece of content. Every test of a proposed monetization hook is the same question: *can a free player get here eventually?*
